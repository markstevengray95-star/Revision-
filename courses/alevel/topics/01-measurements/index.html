    Measurement & data-analysis benches

    Practise realistic decisions that sit underneath all A-level practical work: repeated readings, instrument choice, uncertainty, graphing and evaluation.

    Timing bench · reduce percentage uncertainty

    Compare timing a single oscillation with timing many oscillations and dividing by the count.

    00.00 s
    Evaluation focus: time several oscillations, use a fiducial marker, repeat measurements and quote a realistic uncertainty.

    Results and uncertainty

    OscillationsTotal t / sPeriod T / sΔT / s% uncertainty

    Micrometer bench · repeat and check zero error

    Measure a wire at several positions and orientations. A controllable zero error lets you distinguish random variation from a systematic offset.

    0.50 mm
    Evaluation focus: close the micrometer before use, correct any zero error, take repeated readings in different positions/orientations and do not overtighten.

    Take at least five readings.

    Graph bench · error bars and gradient uncertainty

    Generate a linear data set with adjustable uncertainty, then compare best, steepest and shallowest acceptable gradients.

    Evaluation focus: error bars show uncertainty in individual points. Gradient uncertainty is estimated from the range of acceptable steepest and shallowest lines.

    Instrument chooser · select the most suitable apparatus

    Choose an instrument that gives suitable range and resolution for the measurement. This mirrors the decision-making needed across A-level practical work.

    Decision feedback

    Choose a task, then select an instrument.
    Exam focus: justify the choice using range, resolution and the size of the quantity being measured. More digits on a display do not automatically mean a more accurate result.
