    Generate readings, identify controls, linearise relationships and interpret gradients/intercepts.

    RP5 · Determine wire resistivity

    Measure diameter with a micrometer, vary wire length, measure V and I, calculate R, then use ρ = RA/L.

    AV
    Evaluation focus: measure diameter at several positions and orientations, avoid heating the wire, use a long length range, repeat readings and switch off between measurements.

    Results and analysis

    L / md / mmV / VI / AR / Ωρ / Ω m
    Collect at least four lengths.

    RP6 · Determine emf and internal resistance

    Vary the external resistance and record terminal p.d. and current. Plot V against I: intercept = ε and gradient = −r.

    ε, r
    8 Ω
    AV
    Evaluation focus: use a wide current range, avoid leaving the circuit connected, repeat readings, monitor cell heating and use a high-resistance voltmeter.

    Results and graph

    Rload / ΩI / AV / Vlost volts / V
    Collect at least five load values.
