# AQA Further Mechanics & Thermal Physics Learning Lab

Interactive teaching, simulation, assessment and revision app for **AQA A-level Physics 7408 Section 3.6**.

## Specification coverage

### 3.6.1 Periodic Motion — Paper 1
- radians, angular displacement, angular speed and tangential speed
- centripetal acceleration and centripetal force
- simple harmonic motion, phase and x/v/a graphs
- mass–spring systems and simple pendulum
- SHM energy, damping, forced vibration and resonance
- Required Practical 7

### 3.6.2 Thermal Physics — Paper 2
- internal energy and thermal-energy transfer
- specific heat capacity and continuous-flow heating
- latent heat and phase-change curves
- gas laws and absolute zero
- ideal-gas equations using moles and molecules
- Avogadro constant, Boltzmann constant and molecular mass
- Required Practical 8: Boyle and Charles investigations
- Brownian motion and molecular explanations of gas behaviour
- kinetic-theory assumptions and pressure derivation
- mean molecular kinetic energy and monatomic ideal-gas internal energy

## Final classroom feature set

- 24 sequenced mini-textbook lessons with original diagrams
- detailed equation breakdowns and calculation practice
- 17 interactive simulations with direct manipulation
- live graphs, ruler/protractor/cursor tools, frame stepping and replay
- current-value calculation questions generated from simulations
- guided simulation investigations and predict-then-test challenges
- RP7/RP8 practical-data tools with uncertainty analysis
- adaptive Mastery Hub, spaced retrieval and misconception interventions
- timed topic and mixed assessments with difficulty levels
- repeated-success mastery rule rather than one-answer completion
- equation-memory, unit-conversion and significant-figure trainers
- exam-paper and printable worksheet builders
- local student profiles with autosaved written/numerical work
- CSV, printable report and portable progress-file export/import
- teacher notes, presentation mode, freeze-and-annotate and fullscreen simulation teaching
- side-by-side comparison mode and simulation presets
- English/Mandarin glossary, read-aloud, contrast, text-size and reduced-motion tools
- low-power device optimisation for school tablets/Chromebooks
- AQA specification coverage report and topic dependency map
- installable offline PWA with update notification and issue-report export

## Data model

The app is intentionally **local-first**. Student profiles and progress remain in browser storage. No account, server or database is required. Progress can be moved between devices using the export/import tools.

## Run

Open `index.html` directly, or run:

```bash
npm start
```

The project is static and can be deployed to Vercel, Netlify or another static host.
