    AQA practical skills

    Required Practicals 7 and 8

    Generate data, repeat readings, linearise relationships and practise evaluation.

    RP7 · Mass–spring system

    Time 10 oscillations, calculate T and T², then investigate T² against mass.

    0.30 kg
    Evaluation focus: time many oscillations, use repeats, keep amplitude small, secure the stand, and consider reaction-time uncertainty.

    Results and graph

    m / kg10T / sT / sT² / s²
    Collect at least three readings.

    RP7 · Simple pendulum

    Time 10 oscillations, calculate T² and investigate T² against length.

    Evaluation focus: measure pivot-to-centre length, keep angle small, use a fiducial marker and repeat timings.

    Results and graph

    L / m10T / sT / sT² / s²
    Collect at least three readings.

    RP8 · Boyle's law

    Vary trapped-gas volume at approximately constant temperature and measure pressure.

    Evaluation focus: compress slowly, allow temperature to stabilise, check for leaks and sensor zero error.

    Results and linearisation

    V / cm³p / kPa1/V / cm⁻³pV
    Collect at least five readings.

    RP8 · Charles's law

    Change gas temperature at constant pressure and measure volume after thermal equilibrium.

    Evaluation focus: wait for thermal equilibrium, keep pressure constant and avoid parallax when reading volume.

    Results and graph

    θ / °CT / KV / cm³V/T
    Collect at least five readings.
